# Lsong Individual Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Version 0.1 — English controlling text; Chinese guidance appears below.

**Important:** This CLA is **not automatically imposed on all repositories** under the `lsongdev` account. It applies only to a project that explicitly adopts it, and only to contributions for which the contributor has affirmatively accepted this specific version. Opening a pull request, without that separate affirmative agreement, does not by itself execute this CLA.

## 1. Parties and Contributions

For this agreement, **Maintainer** means Lsong, the individual maintaining the [lsongdev](https://github.com/lsongdev) projects (contact: [hi@lsong.org](mailto:hi@lsong.org)), or a legal successor expressly identified in a project's adoption notice.

**You** means the individual accepting this agreement. A **Contribution** is the copyrightable material you intentionally submit for inclusion in a project that expressly adopts this CLA, after your affirmative acceptance of this version.

## 2. Copyright Ownership and Grant

You retain ownership and copyright in your Contributions.

You grant the Maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as required by applicable law), transferable, and sublicensable copyright license to reproduce, use, modify, distribute, publicly display, publicly perform, and create derivative works of your Contributions, and to sublicense or relicense them under open-source, source-available, proprietary, and commercial terms, including in closed-source products.

This grant does not require you to assign your copyright or prevent you from independently using and licensing your own original work.

## 3. Patent Grant

To the extent you control relevant patent claims, you grant the Maintainer and recipients of its authorized sublicenses a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free patent license to make, use, sell, offer for sale, import, and otherwise transfer your Contribution, but only for patent claims necessarily infringed by your Contribution alone or by its combination with the project as submitted.

## 4. Your Representations

You represent that:

1. You are legally entitled to make these grants.
2. Your Contribution is your original work, or you have identified third-party material and obtained rights sufficient for its inclusion and the grants above.
3. If an employer or another party may own the relevant intellectual property, you have obtained the required authorization.
4. You will identify any known licensing or patent restrictions that could affect acceptance.

Except for these representations, Contributions are provided without warranties.

## 5. No Support Obligation

You are not required to provide future contributions, maintenance, updates, or support.

## 6. Acceptance and Records

This agreement becomes applicable only when a project expressly adopts this CLA and you record an affirmative acceptance for your Contributions, such as an explicit comment on your first relevant pull request stating:

> I have read and agree to the Lsong Individual Contributor License Agreement v0.1 for my contributions to this project.

The maintainer should retain a verifiable record of the contributor identity, project, acceptance, and the exact CLA revision. Later changes to this CLA do not automatically bind existing accepted Contributions.

A checkbox or pull-request template alone does not substitute for a demonstrable affirmative acceptance. Contributions lacking the required rights or acceptance may be declined.
