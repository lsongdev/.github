# Contributor Code of Conduct
# 贡献者行为准则

We appreciate your interest in our open source projects. We have learned a great deal from the open source community, and we are deeply grateful for its spirit of sharing and collaboration. We hope that our work can help others learn and create, just as the work of others has helped us. We look forward to building a more open and thriving community together.

首先，感谢您对我们的开源项目产生兴趣。我们曾从开源社区中学习并收获良多，也始终热爱和感激这种分享知识、互相帮助的文化。我们希望自己的作品也能帮助他人学习与创造，正如前人的成果曾帮助我们一样。期待与您一道共同建设更加开放、繁荣的开源世界。

Our goal is to build an open, inclusive, and welcoming community. Regardless of age, body size, physical ability, race, gender identity and expression, experience, education, economic background, nationality, appearance, or skin color, everyone is invited to participate equally. We do not tolerate harassment, belittling, discrimination, threats, insults, disclosure of private information without permission, or other conduct that undermines mutual respect and honest collaboration.

我们的目标是建设一个开放、包容且友善的社区。在这里，无论年龄、体型、身体机能、种族、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育背景、经济状况、国籍、外貌或肤色，每个人都受到平等的邀请与尊重。我们不容忍任何形式的骚扰、贬低、歧视、威胁、侮辱、未经许可泄露隐私，以及其他破坏相互尊重与真诚协作的行为。

If you would like to participate, please first read the license of the project you wish to contribute to and refer to our [CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/lsongdev/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) guidelines. If the project does not provide a license, you may consult our [license reference](https://github.com/lsongdev/.github/blob/main/LICENSE.md), but it does not automatically grant rights for that project; please confirm the applicable terms with its maintainers. Some projects may separately adopt a [Contributor License Agreement](https://github.com/lsongdev/.github/blob/main/CLA.md), which requires your explicit acceptance.

如果您已经跃跃欲试、希望参与贡献，请先了解具体项目采用的许可证，再参考我们的 [CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/lsongdev/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) 贡献指南。如果项目没有提供许可证，可以先查阅我们的[许可证说明](https://github.com/lsongdev/.github/blob/main/LICENSE.md)，但这并不意味着该项目自动获得授权，请向项目维护者确认适用条款。部分项目可能单独采用[贡献者许可协议](https://github.com/lsongdev/.github/blob/main/CLA.md)，届时需要贡献者明确同意。

This code of conduct will be interpreted and enforced by community leaders and project maintainers in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces. Inappropriate conduct should be reported privately to [hi@lsong.org](mailto:hi@lsong.org). If you discover a security vulnerability, please follow our [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/lsongdev/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) instead.

本行为准则由社区负责人及项目维护者解释和执行，适用于 Issue、Pull Request、讨论区及其他由项目管理的协作空间。如果发现不适当的行为，请通过 [hi@lsong.org](mailto:hi@lsong.org) 私下反馈。如果发现安全漏洞，请按照 [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/lsongdev/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) 中的说明报告。

All reports will be reviewed with care and discretion, with respect for the privacy of those involved. Maintainers may request corrections, remove inappropriate content, or restrict participation when necessary. We will make reasonable efforts to respond, but cannot promise a particular outcome or response time.

所有反馈都将得到审慎的审查，并尽可能尊重相关人员的隐私。维护者可视情况要求整改、删除不适当的内容或限制参与。我们会在必要时尽力回应，但无法保证特定的处理结果或响应时效。
