# Support / 获取帮助

Please use the relevant project's README, issues, or discussions for usage questions and bug reports. Search existing issues before opening a new one.

使用问题与 Bug 反馈请优先查看对应项目的 README、Issues 和 Discussions，并先搜索已有讨论。

Projects are generally provided **as is**, without guaranteed support, maintenance, security updates, response time, or fitness for a particular purpose, except where a separate written contract expressly provides otherwise.

项目通常以**按现状提供（as is）**的方式发布；除非另有明确书面合同，否则不承诺技术支持、持续维护、安全更新、响应时效或适用于特定用途。

Maintainers welcome constructive reports and contributions, but cannot assume responsibility for third-party packages, deployments, paid services, or customer commitments. Contact the party who sold or provided a particular service for its support.

维护者欢迎有建设性的反馈与贡献，但不负责第三方安装包、部署、收费服务或其向客户作出的售后承诺。通过第三方购买或取得服务的用户，应先联系相应提供方。

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting publicly.

安全漏洞请按照 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 私下报告。

For separate commercial licenses or contractual support arrangements, contact [hi@lsong.org](mailto:hi@lsong.org).

独立商业授权或合同支持服务可联系 [hi@lsong.org](mailto:hi@lsong.org)。
