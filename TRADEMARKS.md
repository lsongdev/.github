## Attribution / 署名

You may fork, modify, rename, and redistribute software in accordance with the applicable project license. Preserve the copyright and license notices required by that license, and accurately identify material changes.

在遵守具体项目许可证的前提下，您可以 Fork、修改、重命名和再分发软件。请保留协议要求的版权及许可声明，并如实标注重要修改。

## Identity and endorsement / 官方身份与背书

Do not falsely claim that an unofficial fork, product, package, or paid service is the original project's official release or is endorsed or supported by its maintainers.

请勿将非官方分支、产品、安装包或收费服务冒充官方版本，也不要虚假声称获得原项目维护者认可或提供官方支持。

Accurate, non-misleading references to a project's name or origin are welcome. Use of logos or branding in a way that creates confusion about origin or endorsement may require separate permission, to the extent protected by applicable law.

我们欢迎准确、不具误导性的项目名称与来源说明。若徽标或品牌使用方式可能使人混淆产品来源或背书关系，在适用法律提供保护的范围内，可能需要单独许可。
